# Exam Focus: the Teacher's 40-Mark Pattern
This tab is built **exactly around what your teacher told you to focus on**. Every question here is written in the exam's format, with the marks split shown and a full model answer you can reproduce in the paper. Start here in the last days before the exam; use the rest of the site for depth.

## The paper at a glance ⭐⭐⭐
| CO | Marks | Type | What the question will ask (teacher's importants) | Practise here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CO3** | **10** | Numerical | **Carbon accounting**: calculate Scope 1, 2, 3 for **different scenarios**, draw a **comparison table** of the scopes, say which scenario is better **at scope level and overall**, then which scope is **huge**, what **actions** mitigate it and **why** | [CO3 10-markers](e1-co3-carbon-10-markers.md) |
| **CO4** | **10** | Numerical | **Distribution networks, logistics cost, distance criteria**: scenarios with different costs; (1) objective function, (2) decision variables, (3) assumptions, (4) total cost from transport, inventory, facility, (5) compare and select, (6) interpretation + **sustainable technologies** (IoT, blockchain, AI route optimisation) | [CO4 10-markers](e2-co4-network-10-markers.md) |

## Where to spend your study time ⭐⭐⭐
Match study time to marks: **CO3 and CO4 together are half the paper.**
| Priority | CO | Share of the paper | Suggested share of revision time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CO3 carbon accounting | 25% | 25–30% |
| 1 | CO4 network design | 25% | 25–30% |
| 2 | CO2 supplier selection | 17.5% | 15–20% |
| 2 | CO5 performance measurement | 17.5% | 15–20% |
| 3 | CO1 + CO6 theory | 15% | 10% |

In the paper, spend time in proportion to marks too: roughly **a quarter of your time on each 10-marker**, **a sixth on each 7-marker**, and **a few minutes on each 3-marker**. Do the 3-markers first if you know them cold (quick marks), then the two 10-markers.

## The answer template for every numerical ⭐⭐⭐
1. **Given / assumptions**: list the data; write any assumption in bold (factor, base value, unit).
2. **Formula**: write it before the numbers.
3. **Working**: one line per item, **units on every number**; convert kg → t, kWh → MWh.
4. **Result table**: totals, shares, ranks.
5. **Interpretation**: what the numbers mean (hotspot, winner, trade-off, sensitivity).
6. **Recommendation**: two or three actions with their effect in numbers, plus one line of course theory.

## The ten numbers and formulas you must not forget ⭐⭐⭐
| # | Item | Value / formula |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diesel | **2.68 kg CO₂ per litre** |
| 2 | Indian grid | **0.70 t CO₂ per MWh** (0.70 kg/kWh) |
| 3 | Natural gas · LPG | 1.9 kg/Sm³ · 3.0 t/t |
| 4 | GWP | CH₄ ≈ 28 · N₂O 273 · SF₆ 25,200 |
| 5 | Emissions | Activity × emission factor; CO₂e = gas × GWP |
| 6 | Network objective | **Z = C_total + λ·E_CO₂ + μ·W_waste** |
| 7 | Centre of gravity | Cx = Σxw ÷ Σw, Cy = Σyw ÷ Σw; load-distance = Σ load × distance |
| 8 | Weighted score | Σ weight × score; raw data: lower-better = best ÷ actual × 10 |
| 9 | Logistics total cost | transport (t × km × ₹/t-km) + inventory (t × value × carrying % × days ÷ 365) + facility + λ·CO₂ |
| 10 | Scorecard KPIs | OTIF % · defect PPM · kg CO₂e per unit · RE % · waste diverted % · LTIFR = LTIs × 10⁶ ÷ hours; KPI score = actual ÷ target (or target ÷ actual) × 100 |
