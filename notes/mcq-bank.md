## Lecture 9 — Technology adoption and trade-offs (quiz) ⭐⭐⭐

**Q1. According to Lecture 9, the relationship "technology adoption → operational performance → sustainability performance" is: (a) Always linear (b) Not guaranteed to be linear (c) Always negative (d) Irrelevant to SSCM**
**Answer: (b) Not guaranteed to be linear** — potential benefit ≠ realised benefit.

**Q2. Which of these can technology INCREASE in a supply chain? (a) Transport emissions only (b) Electronic waste and energy consumption (c) Inventory only (d) Traceability only**
**Answer: (b) Electronic waste and energy consumption** — along with capital outlay, cyber risk and digital-infrastructure CO₂.

**Q3. Lecture 9 adds which "4th pillar" to sustainable network design? (a) Governance (b) Innovation (c) Resilience (d) Technology**
**Answer: (c) Resilience** — visibility, agility, recovery, response, assurance.

**Q4. The framework "environmentally friendly + socially ethical + economically viable" is attributed to: (a) Kaplan–Norton (b) Carter–Rogers (c) Elkington (d) Klöpffer**
**Answer: (b) Carter–Rogers**

**Q5. At the top of the Supply Chain 4.0 pyramid is: (a) ERP (b) Cloud platforms (c) AI / GenAI (d) Blockchain**
**Answer: (c) AI / GenAI** — ERP/digital integration is the base.

**Q6. At the base of the Supply Chain 4.0 pyramid is: (a) IoT (b) ERP / digital integration (c) Digital twins (d) Robotics**
**Answer: (b) ERP / digital integration**

**Q7. In the problem–technology table, "empty trucks" are addressed by: (a) Blockchain (b) AI route optimisation (c) Digital product passports (d) Robotics**
**Answer: (b) AI route optimisation** — lower fuel/CO₂.

**Q8. "Product recalls" are addressed in the table by: (a) Blockchain — faster traceability (b) IoT — less food waste (c) Digital twins (d) AI forecasting**
**Answer: (a) Blockchain — faster traceability**

**Q9. "Food spoilage" is best tackled by: (a) RFID for returns (b) IoT sensors (c) ERP (d) Robotics**
**Answer: (b) IoT sensors** — less food waste.

**Q10. Digital twins in the table provide: (a) Material traceability (b) Scenario planning / better network decisions (c) Supplier ESG monitoring (d) Faster recalls**
**Answer: (b) Scenario planning / better network decisions**

**Q11. Which is NOT one of Lecture 9's five hidden trade-offs? (a) Efficiency vs environmental footprint (b) Traceability vs energy (c) Price vs quality (d) Digital inclusion vs supplier exclusion**
**Answer: (c) Price vs quality** — the five are efficiency vs footprint, traceability vs energy, visibility vs privacy, global optimisation vs local resilience, digital inclusion vs supplier exclusion.

**Q12. "Technology can improve Tier-1 sustainability while worsening Tier-2/Tier-3 inclusion" describes: (a) Visibility vs privacy (b) Digital inclusion vs supplier exclusion (c) Global optimisation vs local resilience (d) Traceability vs energy**
**Answer: (b) Digital inclusion vs supplier exclusion**

**Q13. Blockchain's CO₂ footprint and high energy needs illustrate the trade-off: (a) Traceability vs energy (b) Efficiency vs footprint (c) Visibility vs privacy (d) Inclusion vs exclusion**
**Answer: (a) Traceability vs energy**

**Q14. In the technology–sustainability matrix, high business value + low sustainability impact = (a) Avoid (b) Reconsider (c) Optimise (d) Prioritise**
**Answer: (c) Optimise**

**Q15. Low business value + high sustainability impact = (a) Avoid (b) Reconsider (c) Optimise (d) Prioritise**
**Answer: (b) Reconsider**

**Q16. The weight of the Social dimension in the Technology Adoption Score is: (a) 15% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 10%**
**Answer: (b) 20%** — TAS = 25% Eco + 25% Env + 20% Soc + 15% Resilience + 15% Strategic fit.

**Q17. TAS dimensions are each scored on a scale of: (a) 0–10 (b) 1–5 (c) 1–9 (d) 0–100**
**Answer: (b) 1–5**

**Q18. A technology scores Eco 4, Env 4, Soc 3, Res 4, Fit 4. Its TAS is: (a) 3.6 (b) 3.8 (c) 4.0 (d) 3.75**
**Answer: (b) 3.8** — 1.0 + 1.0 + 0.6 + 0.6 + 0.6.

**Q19. Net Technology Value = Gross benefits − Adoption cost − Lifecycle sustainability cost − ___: (a) Tax (b) Risk cost (c) Depreciation (d) Opportunity cost**
**Answer: (b) Risk cost**

**Q20. The Walmart–IBM mango pilot reduced provenance tracing from 7 days to: (a) 2.2 hours (b) 2.2 minutes (c) 2.2 seconds (d) 22 seconds**

**Q23. Step 4 of the Tech-Sustain framework is: (a) Define the problem (b) Evaluate total lifecycle impact (c) Pilot → measure → scale (d) Establish the baseline**
